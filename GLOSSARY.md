# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses that Instructors build and Learners take. This glossary covers the people involved, buying and taking courses, and getting money back after a Purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who takes courses on Cadence, having bought one for themselves or claimed a Seat.
_Avoid_: Student, User

**Instructor**:
A person who owns and builds courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform. Platform Admins are the only people who decide Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Team Admin**:
A person who buys and manages a Team Licence. A Team Admin is only a Learner on its course if they also have an Enrollment there.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

### Courses

**Enrollment**:
A Learner's right to take one course. A Learner has at most one Enrollment per course, whether it came from an Individual Purchase or a Claimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Place, Access

**Lapsed**:
A Learner whose Enrollment on a course has ended.

**Watched**:
The seconds of video a Learner has actually played in a course: the furthest point reached in each lesson, added up. Not lessons opened.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course. It is either an Individual Purchase or a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Individual Purchase**:
A Purchase a Learner makes for themselves. It gives the buyer an Enrollment on the course.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase a Team Admin makes for others. It grants Seats, not an Enrollment for the buyer.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One claimable slot on a Team Licence. Claiming it gives a Learner an Enrollment on the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat no Learner has claimed yet.

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a Learner has claimed. It belongs to that Learner, so its Team Licence can no longer be refunded.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for one specific Purchase. A Purchase can have several over time, but only one Open Refund Request at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet. While it is open, the buyer keeps their Enrollment and a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats can't be claimed.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. It ends what the Purchase granted, the buyer's Enrollment or the Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats, and leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down. The buyer keeps what the Purchase granted, gets no Refund, and may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The full amount the buyer paid, returned after their Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose Refund Request has been approved, where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

### Refund rules

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase in which its buyer can raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Refund period, Grace period

**Watch Limit**:
One hour of Watched time on a course, after which the buyer of an Individual Purchase can no longer raise a Refund Request for it.
_Avoid_: Watch cap, Viewing threshold

**Completion Limit**:
Half of a course's lessons completed, after which the buyer of an Individual Purchase can no longer raise a Refund Request for it.
